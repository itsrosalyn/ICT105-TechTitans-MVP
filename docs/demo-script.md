# Demo Script

## Demo Objective
Demonstrate how AttendQR provides a secure and efficient attendance system by allowing students to check in using QR codes with GPS verification, while enabling lecturers to manage attendance records and monitor attendance performance through a dashboard.

| **1** | Landing Page                        | Introduce AttendQR, explain the problem of manual attendance and proxy attendance, and identify students and lecturers as the target users.          | **FR-01**               | Understand the project's purpose and target users.                                      |

---

## Closing Statement

AttendQR simplifies attendance management by combining QR code scanning with GPS verification to provide a faster, more reliable, and more secure attendance process. The system benefits both students and lecturers by reducing manual work, preventing proxy attendance, and providing real-time attendance tracking and monitoring.
