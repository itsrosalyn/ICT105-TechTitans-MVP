# Sales Scenario

## 1. Project Title

AttendQR – QR Code Attendance System with GPS Verification

## 2. Target User / Customer

- **Primary user:** University students
- **Secondary user:** University lecturers
- **Decision-maker or admin role, if any:** Faculty administrators or university management

## 3. Situation / Context

At the beginning of every class, lecturers spend several minutes checking attendance manually. Students also have to wait while attendance is recorded. Sometimes, students ask friends to mark attendance for them, making the records inaccurate.

## 4. Pain Point

Manual attendance is slow, reduces teaching time, and may allow proxy attendance. Lecturers also need extra time to correct attendance records.

## 5. Product Introduction

A lecturer introduces AttendQR as a simple attendance system. The lecturer generates a QR code, students scan it with GPS verification, and the attendance is recorded instantly.

## 6. Value Message

**For university students and lecturers who experience slow and inaccurate attendance recording, our AttendQR system helps them record attendance quickly and securely by using QR code check-in with GPS verification.**

## 7. Sales Conversation / Pitch Moment

**Team Member:** "Do you spend several minutes taking attendance every class? AttendQR can help. Lecturers generate a QR code, students scan it with GPS verification, and attendance is recorded instantly. It saves class time, improves accuracy, and makes attendance management much easier."

**Lecturer:** "That sounds useful. I'd like to see how it works."

**Team Member:** "Great! Please try our prototype and let us know your feedback."

## 8. Expected User Action

- Try the demo
- Give feedback

## 9. Related Requirements

- **FR-01** – User Login
- **FR-03** – QR Code Generation
- **FR-05** – QR Code Check-in
- **FR-06** – GPS Verification
- **FR-08** – Attendance History
