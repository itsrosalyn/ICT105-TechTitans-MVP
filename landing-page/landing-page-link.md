## Notes
The landing page (built with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript and hosted on Netlify) introduces the AttendQR system and guides users to the live prototype. When users click **"Try the Demo"**, they are taken to the **Live Prototype** section of the landing page. From there, they can click **"Open Prototype Demo"** to launch the interactive prototype.

In the prototype, new users can **create an account** before logging in. After logging in, users can experience the complete attendance workflow, including QR code scanning, GPS verification, attendance confirmation, attendance history, and lecturer dashboard features. The landing page also explains the system's problem, solution, key features, and expected benefits before directing users to the prototype.
