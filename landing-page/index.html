Problem

Manual roll calls don't hold up

Manual roll calls are slow and easy to game — one student can answer for a friend who never walked into the room, leaving lecturers with attendance numbers they can't fully trust.

Solution

Two checks, one scan

Students scan a session QR code, which also checks their GPS position, so a record only saves when both the code and location match the classroom. Lecturers track the 80% rule from a live dashboard.

Who it's for

Students and lecturers, one login

Built for university students who check in to class sessions, and lecturers who need to run those sessions and confirm the 80% attendance rule — both log in from the same page and land on the view built for their role.

Expected value

Numbers both sides can trust

Students get a check-in that takes seconds and a clear record of their own attendance. Lecturers get attendance numbers they can trust, without manually cross-checking a paper sheet against who was actually in the room.

Key prototype features

Four steps, from scan to dashboard.

1

QR check-in

Students scan a session code to start their attendance record.

2

GPS verification

The scan is only accepted inside the classroom's location radius.

3

Attendance history

Students see their own check-ins and running attendance percentage.

4

Lecturer dashboard

Open a session, watch check-ins arrive, flag anyone under 80%.
